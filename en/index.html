The last mile of your ERP

When you implemented your ERP, the project went as far as they all go: accounting, purchasing, central inventory. Then the ribbon was cut and the consultant left.

But your company doesn't end there. It continues on the street with your salespeople, in the warehouse with the stock counts, in the field with the machinery, in finance where someone transcribes invoices by hand. That stretch was never built.

Koddo builds exactly that stretch: the apps, connections and dashboards that take your system to the furthest corner of your operation. Without replacing your ERP. Without never-ending projects.

How we cover that mile

Four ways to take your system where it doesn't reach today.

Apps for your team

Your people record from their phones — on the street, in the warehouse or in the field, with or without signal — and everything flows straight into your SAP B1. No paper, no transcription.

Dashboards for decisions

Sales, inventory and operations on a single screen, updated in real time. No more waiting for Monday's Excel to know how your company is doing.

Connections to your SAP B1

Your invoicing system, your bank, your e-commerce, your weighing scale — whatever is moved by hand between systems today, we connect so it flows on its own.

Applied intelligence
