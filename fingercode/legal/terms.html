2. Description of the service

FingerCode is an iPhone application that analyzes the user's muscle memory. The app captures repetitions of a sequence entered manually by the user and organizes the statistics (frequency, position probability, statistical confidence) of that data. It does not crack, does not guess and does not deliver certainties: it presents statistical data for the user to interpret and decide on their own judgment.

The application works completely offline and transmits no data outside the device.
